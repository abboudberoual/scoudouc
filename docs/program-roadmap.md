# Program roadmap: the Scoudouc vehicles

Five vehicles, each adding exactly one capability. All under 1,500 g, all with a backup parachute, none flies before the bench stages. Slide deck: "Scoudouc Program Roadmap" (Claude artifact). Dates are planning targets set November 2026.

| Vehicle | Height | Body | Mass | Motors | New capability | Target | Milestones | Season |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scoudouc 0 "Bench" | 30 cm | 66 mm | ~300 g | C6 | Measure thrust; gimbal holds a hanging body upright | Thrust curves for 3+ motors; recovers to vertical in <1 s | M3–M5 | Feb–Apr 2027 |
| Scoudouc 1 "Hop" | 40 cm | 66 mm | ~350 g | 1× D12 | Finless flight steered by the gimbal | 10–20 m hop within 10° of vertical, chute recovery | M6 | May–Jun 2027 |
| Scoudouc 2 "Land" | 60 cm | 66 mm | ~900 g | E12 up, E12/F15 down | Landing burn, fixed shock-absorbing legs | ~30 m up, lands upright within 15 m | M7–M8 | Jul–Oct 2027 |
| Scoudouc 3 "Fold" | 72 cm | 74 mm | ~1,200 g | E/F up, F down | Folding legs, roll control | ~100 m, within 5 m, roll <30°/s | — | 2028 |
| Scoudouc 4 "Return" | 85 cm | 74 mm | ~1,400 g | F up, F down | Pad-to-pad precision, reuse, telemetry | ~150 m, within 1 m, 3 flights on one airframe | — | 2028 |

Rules: under 1,500 g; always a backup chute; one new thing per vehicle; bench before flight; cheap enough to lose; the owner can explain every system before it flies.
