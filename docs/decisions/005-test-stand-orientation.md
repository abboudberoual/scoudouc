# 005 — Thrust stand orientation: nozzle up

**Date:** 2026-11-02  **Status:** accepted

## What we decided
The Scoudouc 0 thrust stand holds the motor nozzle-UP in a printed cup sitting on the load cell on a weighted plywood base. Thrust pushes down into the cell and the ground.

## Why
- Securing is just mass (10 kg, about 3× the 30 N peak) and two stakes; the test presses the stand into the ground instead of trying to lift it.
- No guide rods, no blast deflector, no top beam. Fewer parts, fewer ways to lose force before it reaches the cell.
- Exhaust goes up and away from the cell, wiring and people.

## What we gave up
The ejection charge (forward end of the motor) now fires downward into the cup floor. Nozzle-down and horizontal stands have the same problem in a different direction, because thrust always pushes the forward end toward the cell. Mitigations: 1–2 mm aluminium disc on the cup floor, two base vent notches, prefer plugged or zero-delay motors for stand tests, keep electronics 1 m from the notches, expect one extra downward pulse in the data after the delay.
