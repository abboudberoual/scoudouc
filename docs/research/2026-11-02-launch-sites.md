# Launch and test sites near Montréal (2026-11-02, haiku, ~118k tokens)

- Low-power flights (≤160 N·s, ≤1,500 g) from owner-permitted private land need no Transport Canada authorization. But the legal "model rocket" definition requires a parachute or similar recovery device, so the lander must carry a backup chute.
- Montréal SIM must confirm whether motors count as pyrotechnic pieces. No bylaw mentions model rockets.
- Explosives amendment SOR/2026-206 (Oct 2026) changes motor storage rules; check before ordering.
- Minimum site size: A 30 m, B 60 m, C 120 m, D 150 m, E–G 300 m. Tethered/static tests: treat as launches. Backyards do not qualify for E–F.
- No club field near Montréal; CQF launches 3–4×/year in central Quebec and Lac-Saint-Jean.
- Plan: tune and hop on C/D motors at a 150 m field; E/F at a 300 m farm field with written permission or at CQF launches.
