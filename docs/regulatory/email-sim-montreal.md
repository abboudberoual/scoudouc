## English translation

**Subject:** Inquiry: recreational rocket motors

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am an amateur rocketry enthusiast using solid-propellant motors. My motors are certified by Natural Resources Canada, and I use them under the safety code of the Canadian Association of Rocketry. Launches take place outside the City of Montréal, on private land with the landowner's permission.

I would like to know:

1. Are rocket motors of this type considered "pyrotechnic articles" that require authorization from the Service de sécurité incendie de Montréal?
2. Does any rule apply to storing at home, in Montréal, up to six single-use motors?

Thank you for your help. You can reply to me at [email] or at [phone].

Sincerely,

[First Last]
[Address in Montréal]
